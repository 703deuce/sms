## Platform targets

### tiktok
- Aspect 9:16 full bleed default. Hook in first 1-2s. Target loudness -14 LUFS integrated.
- Captions: high contrast, 2-3 words per line, bottom safe zone.

### shorts
- Aspect 9:16. Slightly longer hook tolerance; end card optional.
- Captions: readable on mobile; avoid covering faces in speaker_follow.

### reels
- Aspect 9:16 or 4:5 when user selects; rhythm-forward cuts; music duck under speech.
