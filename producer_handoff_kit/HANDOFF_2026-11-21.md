## 2. Standing rules (Rick, current)

These are the rules actually in force on November 21, 2026. The Producer skill file `skills/motion-studio-production/RULES_FROM_RICK.md` holds the production-craft rules (including the November 20 additions). The list below is the full operating set a new Chief or Producer must keep.

1. **Approval.** Only Rick's own words count. Producer relays never approve, except a stop.
2. **Never raise commercial rights, licensing, or copyright.** Never gate or disable a feature over them. Rick handles that himself.
3. **No Cursor or cloud-agent code changes** unless Rick asks the bot to make the change. Write a self-contained spec in `/workspace/handoff_for_chatgpt/` (copies live in `specs/` in this kit) for Rick to hand to ChatGPT.
4. **Post-render QA is technical only:** crash, wrong frame count or duration, missing audio, corrupt file, wrong resolution. Never fail or warn the customer or the team over design or "look" checks such as black frames. A real technical render failure auto-retries. (Pre-render craft review in `qa.md` is still how the Producer judges the composed film *before* submit; it must not become a post-render gate or a customer-facing warning.)
5. **Paid ElevenLabs music is ON for every job automatically.**
6. **Send the viewable MP4 as soon as it's rendered**, before the editable files finish uploading.
7. **Render on the sms server (self-hosted), not Modal, for now.** Later a dedicated render server. Modal stays installed only as a fallback and stays disabled unless Rick says otherwise.
8. **Test jobs:** act as a customer and submit through the Transovo front end, in Rick's words. Transovo's agent does research, writing and editing. Transovo has no password login (the login button goes straight in).
9. **Real customer jobs render automatically** when the producer finishes (SaaS, no per-job OK). Only test or manual paid runs, and retries after a failure, need Rick's OK.
10. **Editing preferences.** Clips where people talk keep their original audio, with the score ducked under narration and speech. Music videos are case by case. Design must be premium and professional, never minimal or template-looking. YouTube may be used for stills or screenshots with **no YouTube logo**; never use YouTube video footage.
11. **Don't censor the subject.** Include what the story is actually about (including violent, explicit or speculative material) unless the customer asks to leave it out. Attribute claims to sources. Pick the angle from what the subject is actually known for.
12. **Footage share.** In real-subject films (documentaries, stories, recaps), real video clips should fill at least 50% of runtime (a bit more if possible) after searching all non-YouTube sources. If footage truly is not available, still make the film with photos and graphics and note the lower clip share. Never fail or stop a job for insufficient clips.
13. **Music level.** Under narration, music must sit at least 18 dB below the voice (measure before render). Choose song-with-drums or drumless score per film; serious or scary subjects usually get score.
14. **Voice.** Customers can clone their own voice in Customize ("My voice"). Rick's cloned voice is **"Mine"** (ElevenLabs voice id `AMrklrbQ5abmjLi7qvnt`). Verified end to end on November 20–21, 2026 on job `msaj_6d1ab72d` (Diddy vs Ray J).
15. **Rick is usage-conscious.** Don't poll unless needed. After submit, run `status.sh` at most once; do not loop.
16. **Check sms disk before and during deploys.** Above about 90%, prune unused Docker builds and images (never anything running), or Coolify deployments fail.
17. **Never put passwords, keys, or tokens in chat.** Never commit them. Mention secret files by path and variable names only.
18. **Creator UX.** A single phase; Start goes straight to production; Quick Create (a rename) and Customize.

November 20 rule notes in `memory/rules_20261120_lil_jeff_notes.md` were confirmed in Rick's own words and are already applied in `RULES_FROM_RICK.md` (§2.14–2.16, §3.8–3.9, §5.6–5.8) and `film-pipeline.md`.

## 3. How rendering works now vs before

**Before (through November 19, 2026).** The Producer routine auto-submitted films to **Modal** (CPU, with fan-out). `submit.sh <JOB> cpu` packaged the film, uploaded it to Modal, and Modal rendered, QA'd, published the master and completed the job. The box path (`bg_delivery/`) was fallback when Modal was disabled or submit failed twice.

**Now (November 20–21, 2026, current).** Rendering runs on the **sms server** through a private Coolify app, `motion-render-worker`. The Producer still runs the same command:

```bash
python3 /workspace/motion_kit/modal/make_bundle.py --job-dir <J> --check-page   # until "ok": true
bash /workspace/motion_kit/modal/submit.sh <J> cpu
# then at most one: bash /workspace/motion_kit/modal/status.sh <J>
```

`submit.py` defaults to `RENDER_BACKEND=self_hosted`. The script registers the bundle with the job-scoped assets API, then calls `https://transovo.ai/api/motion-studio/agent/jobs/:id/start-render`. The web app forwards the job internally to the private worker (`motion-render:8098`). The Producer does **not** talk to the render host and does not need `RENDER_HOST_SECRET` or `RENDER_WORKER_URL`.

Modal is **disabled** (`/workspace/motion_kit/modal/.modal_disabled`, or `MODAL_DISABLED=1`). It is kept only as an explicit fallback (`RENDER_BACKEND=modal` or `MOTION_MODAL_FALLBACK=1`) and must not be used without Rick's OK. Config lives at `~/.modal.toml` (profile `transovo`); CLI at `/workspace/motion_kit/modal/venv/bin/modal`.

The producer reports `complete` to the coordinator once the render is *submitted* (the self-hosted worker, like Modal before it, finishes QA, uploads the master, publishes the editable package and completes the job). Do not call `/complete` or `approveMaster` on a live self-hosted job. Do not run `bg_delivery` at the same time as a self-hosted submit.

Expect roughly 9–10 minutes of render time per film minute on the current CPU worker. A later dedicated render server is planned; do not change this path until Rick asks.

## 6. How to rebuild from zero

1. Clone this kit from `master` as shown at the top of this file.
2. Run the bootstrap (idempotent; never prints secret values):

   ```bash
   bash /workspace/sms_producer_kit/producer_handoff_kit/setup/bootstrap_producer_box.sh
   # optional, long, ~8 GB of engines:
   INSTALL_ENGINES=1 bash /workspace/sms_producer_kit/producer_handoff_kit/setup/bootstrap_producer_box.sh
   ```

3. Re-provide secrets into the paths in §4 (values from Rick via a secure form, never chat). Mode 600. Confirm presence by variable **name** only.
4. Install or repair engines if needed:

   ```bash
   bash /workspace/motion_engines/_install/check_engines.sh --fix --force
   ```

   Core engine pins are in `engines/pins.txt`. `check_engines.sh --fix` also handles HyperFrames 0.9.52 and the core five clones listed in the bootstrap.
5. Recreate the **Motion Studio Producer** bot from `profile/profile.json` if it does not already exist. Load memory from `memory/MEMORY_UPDATES_2026_11_19.md` and `memory/producer_memory_and_routines.md`. Load the webhook routine from `routine/transovo-motion-studio-production.md` (the November 21 supersession at the top is the live render path).
6. Wire the coordinator: producer worker `msp-producer-1` must point at that routine's webhook. App 1 must have `MOTION_STUDIO_COORDINATOR_WEBHOOK_KEY` set to the matching key. Box files: `/workspace/secrets/producer_webhook.env` and `/workspace/secrets/motion_runner.env`.
7. Confirm `/workspace/motion_kit/modal/.modal_disabled` exists so jobs go self-hosted. Confirm `submit.sh` / `submit.py` default `RENDER_BACKEND=self_hosted`.
8. Confirm App 1 is the Nixpacks root build (`/nixpacks.toml`) and that the private render worker is healthy (`motion-render:8098`, no public port). The producer submits only to `https://transovo.ai`.
9. Smoke-check by acting as a customer on transovo.ai (login button goes straight in): Quick Create or Customize, then watch that the job reaches the producer webhook and `submit.sh` enqueues on the self-hosted worker. Don't poll in a loop.
