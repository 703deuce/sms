- **Live delivery is ON (Rick, Nov 18; Transovo master `5dbe6b721`).** First check the job: when `job.delivery.mode` is `"live"` (or `productionInstructions` say not to render or upload a master), follow **"Live delivery"** below and skip every render/master/editor-base/stage-approved/publish-editable step in this file. No plan gets an automatic MP4: the customer views the film live in the browser, and the MP4 is rendered only when they press Export, by Transovo's own renderer on regular CPU. Captions are always a separate editable track, never burned in, with no burn-in option at export. Scenes play from the main Transovo domain in sandboxed iframes. Only a job that does NOT carry `delivery.mode:"live"` keeps the older steps (render the master once after approval, watch it, make `editor-base.mp4`, stage and publish).
- **Progress on real transitions only.** Stages: research, story, voice, design, scenes, review, fixing, rendering, delivering (a live job has no `rendering` stage; it goes review → delivering). Report the scene number while building and completed/total files while uploading (publish-editable.mjs does the upload counts itself).
- **Review.** No rendering until everything is approved (Rick, Nov 18). Review in the browser with still screenshots (`stillBrowser` or the engine's still command): full-size first, busiest and last frame of each scene, plus wherever something changes. After a correction, capture only the affected scene and its boundary frames. For movement or sync, step stills through the section or play it live in the browser; never encode a clip to review. Browser engines are always reviewed with browser stills. The non-browser engines (Cinematic Skill, Manim, Natron, any existing Node Skia script) are checked with the exact still recipes in SKILL.md Stage 5 step 9; before approval nothing beyond the exact frame or few seconds being checked is rendered. Final full watch: on a live job, play the whole film in the live browser player (see "Live delivery"); on a job without `delivery.mode:"live"`, render the master once and watch it before delivery.
- **Frame packs.** `$K/producer/lib/bundle.mjs` + `frame-packs.mjs` (installed together) pack numbered PNG/JPEG/WebP frames losslessly into `*.frames` sources (≤16 MB each) when bundles are built. The deterministic runner and sim read packs. A 10 s, 5-scene film is about 41 editable assets instead of 336.
- **Render-once publish path (only jobs WITHOUT `delivery.mode:"live"`)** (replaces live `publish-portable.mjs` uploads):
  1. Build as in SKILL.md Stage 7 step 3 (`build-editable-project.mjs`), then QA.
  2. Validate: `node $K/producer/bin/publish-portable.mjs --dir …/editable/out --dry-run` must PASS (no network). Never run publish-portable.mjs live any more.

  4. Publish with the job URL and job token in the environment (from `secrets/`, never on the command line or in chat): `MOTION_STUDIO_JOB_URL=… MOTION_STUDIO_JOB_TOKEN=… node $K/producer/bin/publish-editable.mjs /workspace/motion_durable/<jobId>/approved /workspace/motion_durable/<jobId>/approved/bundles.json --complete`. It uploads the master first and marks it Video ready, then 8 files at a time with retries, reuses committed receipts, publishes the portable version only after every asset is ready, then completes with the master's measured duration. It pings the job checkpoint every 60 s while it runs (a script HTTP call, no agent turns).
  5. If interrupted (restart, 5xx), re-run step 4 unchanged; it resumes from receipts. Don't rebuild or re-render. For recovery from an earlier job on the same project set `MOTION_STUDIO_RECOVERY_SOURCE_JOB=<old jobId>`.
- **Lease.** The creative lease stays 30 min (server env `MOTION_STUDIO_CREATIVE_LEASE_MS=1800000`) and every job-token call renews it. Do not schedule agent-level heartbeats; normal progress posts at stage changes are enough.

## Live delivery (`job.delivery.mode:"live"`; Rick, Nov 18; contract in `delivery-contract.md` §0)

The agent's job ends at an approved, live-playable, editable project. Nothing about how scenes are designed or reviewed changes; only the final watch and the packaging change.

1. **Build and review exactly as before** (Stages 4b–5): browser stills at exact times, non-browser engines with their still recipes, no encode before approval, no cap on fix rounds. Footage stays inside the browser scene as `video` layers. Every title and graphic keeps its `startMs`/`endMs` active window. A non-browser engine's approved picture (Cinematic Skill, Manim, Natron, a Node Skia script) comes in as a plate: render ONLY that plate once after its stills are approved, as prepared media for a `video` layer, never a scene clip of the whole composite and never the film master.
2. **Write the live spec.** `editable/project.json` (the same spec as before: `title`, `format`, `theme`, `fonts`, `audio.file` = the approved mix, `scenes[]` of `transovo.layer-scene/1`) plus `live`: `{stems:{main_voice:[{file,label}], adlibs:[…], other_audio:[{file:music},{file:sfx},{file:footage_audio}]}, captions:"<captions.json | .vtt | .srt>"}`. Stems are the full-length 48 kHz WAVs from Stage 6, grouped like song tracks (Rick): Main Voice, Ad-libs, Main Visuals, Text, Other audio. Captions are word-timed cues, never burned in.
3. **Dry run (no network):** `node /workspace/ms_producer_editable/producer/bin/publish-live.mjs --spec /workspace/motion_jobs/<jobId>/editable/project.json --out /workspace/motion_jobs/<jobId>/editable/live --job-dir /workspace/motion_jobs/<jobId> --dry-run`. It builds bundles, stems, the caption file, the export recipe, the producer document and the `transovo.live-project/1` timeline, renders NOTHING, and runs Transovo's own validators locally. It must print `PASS`. The one expected warning is "EXPORT GAP (server)" (Transovo's Export can't yet read a document without a master; that is ChatGPT's fix, not ours).
4. **Final full watch = live browser playback.** Open the built project in the live browser player (`/workspace/live_playback_handoff/sample/player.js`, `serve.mjs`, pointed at the built `editable/live` scenes and stems) and play the WHOLE film from start to end with audio. Check pacing, voice-to-picture sync at hit words, real-clip audio moments, music ducking, every cut (no black flash) and caption timing. Wherever something looks or sounds wrong, take full-size stills at that exact time, fix the scene code, re-check those stills and both sides of any touched cut, then play that section again. Run the scripted audio checks (loudness, true peak) on the stems/mix, not on a rendered master. Never render or encode the film to watch it.
5. **Publish and complete:** the same command without `--dry-run`. The token and job URL are read from `secrets/` (never on the command line or in chat). It uploads 8 files at a time with retries and resume from receipts, confirms every file `ready` with a matching sha256, publishes ONE portable version (`{manifest}` carrying `producerDocument`, `producerAssetMap` and `liveProject`), checks the server returned a version with this live timeline, then calls `/complete {delivery:"live", portableVersionId, durationMs}`. Progress posts "Uploading editable files: n / total" itself and pings the checkpoint every 60 s. Do not run `stage-approved.mjs`, `publish-editable.mjs` or `publish-portable.mjs` on a live job: the server keeps the first version a job publishes, so a non-live publish would block live completion.
6. **If interrupted,** re-run step 5 unchanged; committed files are reused from receipts. If `/portable` succeeded but `/complete` failed, read `editable/live/payload/live-publish-receipt.json` and retry only `/complete` with the same `portableVersionId` and `durationMs`.
7. **Rick's test jobs:** there is no master, so there is no 480p preview copy; send Rick the job id and tell him the film is ready to play in Transovo.

## Clean picture and timed overlays (24de16dd)

- **Two videos.** The reviewed composite (`out/master.mp4`, text and graphics burned in) stays the master for playback and download. Also make the **clean cut** `editor-base.mp4`: the same edit with only the plates/footage (no editable titles, graphics or captions), same length, fps and size, H.264 yuv420p, no audio (audio is its own timeline item). Example for plate scenes: trim each `plate_S<n>.mp4` to its scene duration and concat with ffmpeg. Pass it to stage-approved.mjs with `--editor-base`; the server shows it on the editor canvas and keeps the master for playback.
- **Timed overlays.** Every editable title, graphic and audio item gets its active window in the layer scene: `startMs`/`endMs` (scene-local ms) on the layer in `editable/project.json`, plus keyframes inside that window. Do not make a layer span the whole scene just to hide it with opacity. The gap before/after needs no media. For transitions the user should be able to change, keep both source clips with handles and the cut timing; otherwise bake them into the plate.
- **No render captures in delivery.** Final renders stream frames into the encoder. Keep review stills **outside** the scene folder (e.g. `/workspace/motion_jobs/<jobId>/qa/`); for stills during encoding pass a framesDir outside the scene plus explicit `reviewFrameIndices`, or seek with `stillBrowser`. The bundle builder skips `frames/`, `screenshots/`, `render-cache/` folders and `f_*/frame_*/screenshot_*` stills; publish-editable.mjs refuses a package that still has them. Genuine image-sequence artwork must be declared in buildBundle `sourceFiles`.
- **Video plates.** build-editable-project.mjs still pre-decodes each video layer to a JPEG sequence inside the scene (`media/<plate>_<layer>_frames/00001.jpg…`). These are genuine scene inputs (packed losslessly, ~1 pack per scene) and are allowed.
- **Check before the final render:** move one title's startMs and take browser stills before, during and after the new window; the title must appear only inside it.
