Harbor Home Services — extended knowledge (RAG corpus)

Emergency plumbing service
We offer 24/7 emergency plumbing for burst pipes, major leaks, and no-water situations in Northern Virginia. Emergency visits have a $149 dispatch fee in addition to the repair estimate. If you smell gas, leave the home and call 911 first — we cannot dispatch for active gas leaks.

Service area
We serve Arlington, Alexandria, Fairfax, Falls Church, McLean, and Vienna Virginia. We do not travel to Maryland or DC for standard service calls.

Warranty policy
All repairs include a one-year parts-and-labor warranty. Drain cleaning and diagnostic visits are warranted for 30 days. Warranty does not cover new damage, misuse, or work performed by other contractors.

Membership plan — Home Care Plus
Home Care Plus is $19 per month and includes priority scheduling, 10% off repairs, and one free annual plumbing inspection. Members skip the standard $89 diagnostic fee on the first visit of each year.

Common services we book
Bathroom leak repair, kitchen sink installation, water heater repair and replacement, garbage disposal repair, toilet repair, and whole-home repiping consultations. Most standard visits are 30 minutes on the calendar.

Payment
We accept all major cards, checks, and digital wallets. Payment is due when the work is completed unless you have a commercial account.
