Blue Ridge Realty — Real Estate Front Desk Knowledge Base

## About Us
Blue Ridge Realty is a residential real estate brokerage serving Northern Virginia. We help home buyers find properties and assist sellers with listing and marketing their homes. We connect clients with licensed realtors who guide them through the buying and selling process.

## Service Areas
We serve Woodbridge, Dale City, Lake Ridge, Stafford, Fredericksburg, Manassas, and surrounding Northern Virginia communities.

## Property Types
We work with single-family homes, townhouses, and condominiums.

## What We Do NOT Do On Calls
- We are not a mortgage lender and cannot approve financing on this call.
- We do not have live MLS inventory on phone calls — a realtor follows up with matching listings.
- We cannot guarantee a specific home is available without a realtor checking MLS.
- We do not provide legal or tax advice.

## Consultation Appointments
Callers can schedule a consultation with a licensed realtor through our front desk. Consultations are typically 30 minutes and can be in-person or by phone.

## Office Hours
Monday through Friday 9 AM to 6 PM, Saturday 10 AM to 2 PM. Closed Sunday.

## For Buyers
We collect target areas, property type, budget, and timeline so a realtor can prepare matching options before follow-up.

## For Sellers
We collect property address, selling timeline, and reason for selling so a listing agent can prepare a market analysis.

## For Showings
We coordinate showing requests with the listing agent. Provide the property address or MLS number and preferred tour time.

## Existing Clients
Current clients can leave messages for their assigned agent or request a callback.

## Agent-to-Agent Calls
Other licensed agents can leave coordination messages for our team regarding showings, offers, or referrals.
