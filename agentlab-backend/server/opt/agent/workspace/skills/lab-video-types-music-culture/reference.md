# Type playbook: Music and celebrity culture explainers (full detail)

Source labels in [BRACKETS] resolve in `/workspace/skills/lab-video-edit/craft-sources.md`.

**Hook.** A recognisable face or sound in the first 90 f. People on screen early help [ABCD].

**Structure.** Same as 2.4, plus a timeline spine (dates on screen when spoken).

**Pacing.** Cut to the song's structure: phrases, downbeats, accents. Hold a key image across a phrase [HSM-7]. One beat = 60 ÷ BPM s; at 96 BPM a beat is 0.625 s ≈ 19 f [HSM-7].

**Audio.**
- **Must:** song vocals never sit under narration. Duck a vocal track 20 dB or more, cut to an instrumental section, or pause the narration while the hook plays [JAP-DUCK].
- Music inserts enter and leave on phrase boundaries (house rule).

**Captions.** Lyrics shown only when they are the point. Speech captions per 1.10.

**Graphics.** Names, dates, chart or stream numbers on screen with sources (`log/facts.md`).

**Mistakes:**
- Talking over a vocal.
- Cutting mid-bar.
- Unsourced gossip as fact.
- AI images presented as real people (honesty rule).


## Editor notes for this type
- Edit the music first to phrases and a real ending, lock the big hits to downbeats and let the rest breathe; a montage has its own escalating arc, not a slideshow.
