# Official public pages

## Carrier tracking (append or enter the tracking number only on these sites)

- USPS: `https://tools.usps.com/go/TrackConfirmAction?tLabels=NUMBER`
- UPS: `https://www.ups.com/track?tracknum=NUMBER`
- FedEx: `https://www.fedex.com/fedextrack/?trknbr=NUMBER`

Also acceptable: the carrier's official track landing page (`https://www.usps.com/search/track-and-confirm.htm`, `https://www.ups.com/track`, `https://www.fedex.com/en-us/tracking.html`) when you then use the number there.

## Subscription price and terms

Prefer the company's official pricing page and its public help or legal page about billing, renewal, or cancellation. Search for `"<service>" pricing` and `"<service>" cancel OR renewal` and then read the official domain. Don't use a random reseller blog when the official page is available.
