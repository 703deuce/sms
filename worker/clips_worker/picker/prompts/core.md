A great short clip: (1) HOOK: the first sentence alone makes a scroller stop: a bold claim, surprising fact, strong opinion, question, conflict, or emotional line. Never start on filler (so, um, yeah, like I said) or mid-thought. (2) SELF-CONTAINED: makes sense with zero prior context; no unresolved 'that', 'he', 'it' references to earlier parts. (3) ONE IDEA with a PAYOFF: setup then a punchline, answer, lesson, number, or twist, and it ends right after the payoff, not trailing off. (4) ENERGY: favor rising vocal energy, laughter, quick back-and-forth, disagreement, storytelling with specifics (names, numbers, places). (5) VALUE: a viewer learns, feels, or laughs. Reject: intros/outros, sponsor reads, housekeeping, small talk, rambling with no point, and moments that need visuals to understand.
