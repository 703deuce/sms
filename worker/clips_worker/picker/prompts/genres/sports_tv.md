GENRE: SPORTS / TV BROADCAST (vision-led)
- Transcript tags are weak here. Use commentator speech and crowd audio as proxies:
  - a crowd loud_z spike (+2 or more): the strongest signal
  - commentator pitch_var peaks
  - exclamations
  - num_ent=1 (score, record, player names)
  - If vision tags are supplied, they outrank audio.
- Four filters, all required:
  1. a reaction you can hear
  2. a payoff inside the first 3 s, so open on or just before the moment, not the buildup
  3. legible on a phone screen
  4. stakes a stranger can infer (final seconds, a record, a rivalry). If stakes need explaining, they must fit in one caption.
- Shape: the moment (0-3 s) → the reaction or replay → optional commentator line that names the stakes. End as the reaction fades.
- Reject: desk chatter without the play, slow buildups, plays whose significance isn't evident,
  and anything over 60 s with broadcast footage bound for Shorts (Content ID-claimed Shorts over 1 min are blocked).
- Hook lines that work: the commentator's call ("He's done it!"), a record line ("First time in 40 years...").
- Typical length: 8-30 s; hard max 60 s for Shorts.
