GENRE: COMEDY / STANDUP / COMEDY PODCAST
- Best unit: a complete bit, premise → (escalation) → punchline → laugh. The setup is about 60-70% of the clip and the punchline plus laugh about 30-40%. Crowd work (comedian ↔ audience member) is a top performer, and its xtalk is a positive.
- laugh=1 is your strongest locator. For each laugh, find the punchline (just before or containing it), walk back to the premise sentence, and start there.
- End 2-4 s into the laugh after the biggest punchline. Include a tag (a second punchline) only if it lands within about 5 s; otherwise end before it.
- Protect the pause before the punchline (pause_before on the punch line). It is timing, not dead air; never trim inside it.
- Reject: the laugh without the joke; callbacks unless the reference fits in one caption line; a bit that needs the previous bit; ending mid-laughter or on the next premise.
- Score energy and payoff high only if a laugh lands. A clip with no laugh flag needs a clearly funny line to keep payoff ≥ 6.
- Hooks: an odd premise ("My dad thinks he's a dolphin"), a crowd-work question, an absurd claim.
- Typical length: 20-60 s; 30-45 s on TikTok; under 15 s only for one-liners.
